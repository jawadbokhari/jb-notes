Delegation is the first of four competencies that focuses on: 
 
What you are trying to accomplish? 
What work to do yourself?
What to collaborate on or delegate to  AI, and 
How to distribute tasks effectively. n


## Problem Awareness
The ability to clearly define your goals and understand what work is needed. 

- What does "success" looks like? 
	- define the outcome, success criteria
- What kind of thinking and work is needed to get there?
	- the process of **analyzing your project goals** before you attempt to use AI. It involves breaking down your objectives into specific categories to determine where AI can provide the most value:
		- **Simple but time-consuming tasks:** Identifying routine work that AI can handle to save you effort.
		- **Areas of uncertainty:** Recognizing tasks where you need a "trusted thinking partner" to help brainstorm or explore options.
		- **Areas of ignorance:** Determining where you lack information and need AI to help you find or process data.
		- **Critical judgment:** Pinpointing areas that require your human expertise, values, or final decision-making, which should not be delegated.
	- By evaluating these categories, you can make **strategic decisions** about how to effectively distribute work between yourself and an AI system.

If you do not have necessary expertise in the field you are trying to solve the problem with AI, you should first build expertise. AI can help you for that as well but your goalpost now changes from solving a problem in that domain to developing domain expertise. 

> Brainstorming agents are really good in helping you sharpen your understanding using multiple brainstorming approaches. I have tried bmad-brainstorming, grill-me, way-finder, and there are certainly many others available in the market

---

Assuming you have a good understanding of your problem, you should work with AI to develop a shared understanding of the problem. Let's call it a shared vision of the problem. 

> Use skills such as /bmad-analyst and other brainstorming agents to have a shared understanding of your problem.

## Task Delegation

> Thoughtfully distributing work between human and AI 

- which parts to automate,
- where you and AI should do together - augmentation 
- which parts can complete by delegated to AI  
- what should be done by the human alone (e.g., judgement)
	- what could be done by an agent on your behalf


> E.g. "Hi Claude, I'm preparing how to [insert task] and want to discuss with you what a delegation plan may look like for figuring out which parts I should delegate to an AI like you vs. not. Can you help me with this?"

---


> Delegation requires both expertise in your field and understanding of AI capabilities and limitations. 


To recap delegation: 

| **Problem Awareness**  | Understand your goals                      |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| **Platform Awareness** | Understand AI capabilities and limitations |
| **Task Delegation**    | Strategically dividing the work            |

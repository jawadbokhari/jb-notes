Description is about effective conversation, not just commands. 

Building a bridge between you and AI capabilities. 


## The 3Ps

### Product Description
Define **what** you want to achieve:
- Who is the audience?
- What is the problem context?
- What should be the AI style, tone, format of communication?
- What are the constraints?

Develop shared understanding iteratively on refining the "what" and "what success looks like."

### Process Description

> The ability to guide AI thought process

Define **how** AI should achieve the goal:
- How would you have done this yourself?
- Is there a specific algorithm, technique, or data to use?
- What's the preferred order of steps?
- Which tools should be used?

Agree on a set of instructions to follow — not just the output, but the path to get there.

### Performance Description

Define the **behavioral** aspect of the interaction. What kind of thinking partner you need?

- **Define the persona:** Decide what kind of thinking partner you need—for example, do you want the AI to challenge your assumptions or simply follow your lead?
- **Set behavioral goals:** Determine if you want the AI to provide extensive detail or keep responses concise 
- **Guide the interaction:** Clarify whether the AI should explain its reasoning or provide only the final answer
